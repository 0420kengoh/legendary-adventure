The Renovation Journey

Six stages. Fourteen steps. One standard.

Every step is a standard — not an extra. This is how SRW works on every project, from confirmation to closeout.

  1. 01

    Pre-Entry

    Site kickoff & client sign-off. Every measurement is signed before work starts.

  2. 02

    Entry

    Property protection & sealing — floor, doors and fixtures wrapped before any trade enters.

  3. 03

    Progressing

    Laser-set setting-out, daily supervision and a weekly gate review every Friday.

  4. 04

    Pre-Exit QC

    Internal QC sweep and a function test on every point — we find our own defects before you do.

  5. 05

    Handover

    A room-by-room walkthrough and a premium handover pack — warranties, codes, photos and guides.

  6. 06

    Assurance

    24-month quality assurance with a formal closeout inspection — documented sign-off, on paper.
